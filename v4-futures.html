R math: 1R = full-ladder risk |avg(entry,L1) − SL|. Losses are full −1R (L1 always fills before the stop). Entry-only trades (L1 not filled) are scored at half size, so a "hit 1R" target requires the run to reach that profit — roughly twice the distance. Full @1R closes the whole position at 1R; 50% @1R banks half at 1R and trails the rest on BoS. Hybrid A picks per-trade by fill type: L1 filled → 50% @1R + runner, entry-only → Full @1R (the V3 live default). Hybrid B is the inverse. 2R targets are excluded — V3 already retired them.
