R math: 1R = full-ladder risk |avg(entry,L1) − SL|. Losses are full −1R (L1 always fills before the stop). Entry-only trades (L1 not filled) are scored at half size, so a "hit 1R/2R" target requires the run to reach that profit — roughly twice the distance. Full @NR closes the whole position at NR; 50% @NR banks half at NR and trails the rest on BoS. Hybrid A picks per-trade by fill type: L1 filled → 50% @1R + runner, entry-only → Full @1R (the live default — outperforms on the sample). Hybrid B is the inverse: L1 filled → Full @1R, entry-only → 50% @1R + runner.
