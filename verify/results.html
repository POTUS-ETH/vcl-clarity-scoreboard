Price that retraces far enough to reach 0.085 usually carries on to fib 0 and trips the v3 stop as well — landing in the grey bucket instead. Few trades fall in the gap.

Reading the comparison
Both sets are risk-normalised, so an R is an R: size each trade to the same dollar risk and the numbers compare directly. The V5 stop is tighter, which means more size for the same risk — the leverage property that motivated the model.

The honest verdict: V5's best cell beats the ladder's best cell only in the best case. Full size → BE + trail reaches +0.667R if every unresolved trade survives, against the ladder's best of +0.483R (50% @1R). At the pessimistic end it collapses to +0.046R. The midpoint, +0.356R ±0.80, sits below the ladder — and its interval contains zero, while all four ladder cells exclude it.

So on this evidence V5 is not demonstrably better than what you already ran. It may be, at the optimistic end. The 22 unresolved trades decide it, and they are unresolvable from 1m data — the 15s history needed is past the ~30-day wall.
