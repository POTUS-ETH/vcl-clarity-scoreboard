🧭 New here? How this works
The idea: prop firms give you their money to trade once you pass their test. We run many of these accounts at once and place the same trade on all of them simultaneously (copy trading) — same risk, multiplied payouts. This page is a live mirror of the Notion Prop Firm Rotation Tracker — every button here writes straight back to Notion.
  1. Take a trade → the cards highlighted green are the accounts your next trade gets copied to. When the trade closes, hit + Log trade to record each account's $ result, then Trade complete to rotate to the next cluster.
  2. Each card is one account. The colored bar shows where its balance sits between its floor (lose past this = account terminated) and its next target. Green = safe, amber/red = getting close to the target end.
  3. Passing the test: accounts in Challenge/Verification show a blue profit-target bar. When it fills, a button appears — click it once the firm confirms, and the card flips to Funded.
  4. Getting paid: hit + Log payout request when you withdraw. The account freezes ("on ice") while the firm processes it — it skips its trading turns until the timer runs out or you hit Thaw now (if the money lands early).

📖 Glossary — every term on this page, in plain English
Prop firm
A company that lets you trade their money after you pass their test. You keep most of the profit; they eat the losses. You pay a monthly fee per test attempt.
Copy trading
You place one trade; software instantly mirrors it on every other selected account. One decision, many accounts.
Challenge / Eval
The test phase. Reach the profit target without ever breaking the drawdown rule and the account becomes funded.
Funded / Active
Passed the test. Now trading the firm's real capital and eligible to withdraw profits (payouts).
Profit target (PT)
The profit you must reach to pass the test — $3,000 on a 50k account, $6,000 on a 100k.
Drawdown (MDD)
The maximum you're allowed to lose, measured from your highest point. Cross it and the account is terminated. $2,000 on a 50k, $3,000 on a 100k.
Peak / HWM
High-water mark — the best balance the account ever hit. The drawdown limit trails behind it.
Floor
The balance you must stay above. Starts trailing your peak; once you build enough buffer (the safety net), it locks permanently just above your starting size — after that, profit is pure cushion.
On ice
Our term for the freeze after requesting a payout — the firm takes ~48h (Lucid) or ~5 days (Apex) to process, and the account sits out of the trade rotation until the money clears.
Consistency rule
A one-time gate checked at the moment you pass your eval — no single day can account for more than 50% of your total profit. Same 50% rule for both Apex and Lucid. Once you're funded it's behind you; it doesn't keep getting checked.
Cohort / MA / sA1
Naming for the 25-account build. Each cohort = 1 master (MA, MB…) + 4 subs (sA1–sA4…). The Master Cohort is the final elite group assembled at the end.
MOAT
The war chest. Half of every payout goes here; it buys new accounts, and instantly replaces any funded account lost to a breach.
